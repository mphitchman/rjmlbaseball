Team Usage Totals

These numbers are calculated as follows:

  • SP is the total starts by pitchers on their roster. Each team will need to cover 160 starts; rosters with fewer than 160 starts have their starts highlighted in red in the table below.
  • RP is the total BF by pitchers with an RP rating. A team generally uses around 2000 BF per season. Overcounting appears for pitchers with starts and a relief rating. In practice, a dual pitcher used as a starter and a reliever will have fewer relief BF available due to their starts.
  • PA is the team’s total plate appearances, which generally exceeds 6000 each season.
  • Each position total is calculated by the total number of PA by those players on the roster eligible to play that position. In this table, a player eligible at multiple positions will have all of their PA added to each of their positions, resulting in over-counting.
